2. What information do we process?

When you create a Resell Radar account, we process account information such as email, profile name, authentication data and workspace identifiers. We process the products, inventory, costs, sales and alerts you choose to record. Payment status, plan identifiers and essential payment event references are received from our billing provider; full card numbers are not stored by Resell Radar.

If you explicitly connect an eBay account, we may receive the eBay authorization tokens you consented to share and read-only inventory data such as SKUs and product titles. If order synchronization is activated in a future version, we will update this policy to describe the order fields and buyer data used before enabling that feature. The initial eBay Sandbox preview does not save downloaded inventory data in the workspace.

3. Why do we process these data?

We use account and service data to provide login, inventory tools, analytics, plan entitlements, customer support, fraud prevention and essential service notices. Optional eBay access is used only to retrieve information you explicitly authorize and request. We do not sell eBay user data or use it for targeted advertising.

4. eBay integration and account disconnection

When you select “Connect eBay,” you are redirected to eBay to grant read-only permissions. Resell Radar stores the resulting access and refresh tokens encrypted and associated with your own workspace. You can disconnect eBay in Marketplaces, which deletes the locally stored tokens. You can separately revoke third-party access in your eBay account. Connection does not grant Resell Radar permission to modify listings or submit orders.
